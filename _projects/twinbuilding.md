## Quotations, Invoices, & Receipts

The site includes a document generator for creating quotations, invoices, and receipts. The user enters the client's details and requested services into a form, while a live preview displays the document as it is being prepared. Prices and totals are calculated automatically, and each quotation is assigned a unique number.

Quotation data is saved as JSON and used to generate the corresponding invoice and receipt without requiring the information to be entered again. The generator produces branded PDF documents containing the firm's information, banking details, services, prices, and totals.
