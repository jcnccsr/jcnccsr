## FNÚA Fashion

I worked as a graphic designer for FNÚA, working on the brand identity, packaging, in-store graphics, and digital content.

I created the brand guidelines and logo, along with packaging and branded elements including shopping bags, stickers, garment labels, and custom metallic buttons. I also designed the large window graphic displayed at the company's shop in Souk Al Bahar.

## Brand Identity

Created the FNÚA Fashion logo and developed the brand guidelines to establish a consistent visual identity across the brand's materials.

## Digital Content

Created graphics, videos, animations, and promotional content for the brand's social media channels.

## Physical Identity

Designed the brand's physical materials, including packaging, shopping bags, garment labels, stickers, custom buttons, and the store window graphic at Souk Al Bahar.

I also designed the large window graphic displayed at the company's shop in Souk Al Bahar.
