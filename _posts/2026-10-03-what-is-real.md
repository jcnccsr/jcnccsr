What is real ? What is reality ? How do we know if what we perceived as reality is but a dream ? How do we know we're not in a simulation ? How do we even know what we know ?

These musings could sound like a crazy talk. These could probably give somebody an impression of interesting conspiracies or probably skepticism. That's fair. It seems obvious to anyone with common sense, right ? The world is what we see and feel, smell and hear. That is reality.

But how do we know that's true ? I was told once that a claim is true if it corresponds to reality. The problem is, it doesn't give me new information. And so it just loops back in a circle, leaving me asking the question again, "What is reality ?" 

So what is it ? People would say that it's the universe that exists independent of our minds. This doesn't refute the claims of us being just a brain in a vat, or in a lucid dream, or in a simulation. The vat is still objective, even the computer or whatever that is supplying me the sensations, the perception of reality.

The question is now changed. How do know that what I perceive as reality is the objective reality, the world that is independent of my mind ? Since everything I perceive is through my senses. And all my senses are, to simply put it, electrical impulses that go to my brain, and then my brain process it, interpret it, which make the whole thing, the perception, subjective. I'm back at square one. How do I know, that the things perceived by my senses were not electrical impulses made by the vat's simulated world, and my brain is just interpreting it as the world. Or else, I'm just a simulated character in this simulated world running on some kind of computer of some pan-dimensional beings ?

What comes to mind is a trap, and I'm inside of it. Am I the brain, or the brain is my trap that I can't get out of ? My computer, my keyboard, all these things I think I know were seen, touched, felt, and smelled by my senses, which were then turned into some kind of electrical signals that were then interpreted by my brain as "clickity" and "thock", and that tactile sensation when my finger pressed the keys, that gives me pleasure.
