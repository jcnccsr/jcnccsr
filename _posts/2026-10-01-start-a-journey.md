## A Teacher Becomes a Second Mother

What is a better way to start a journey of learning programming than finding a good teacher. And the best teacher for learning how to talk to a computer, is a computer itself ! Wow, how fun is that, being taught by a computer. I wonder if that will make the statement "C is my mother language" meaningful.

## AI Can Teach

Can attest to this. In just a few days, I can now write small programs to solve GPT's challenges without looking up and asking follow-up questions, and that includes problems that uses pointers. Cool right ? Should school businesses be worried ?
